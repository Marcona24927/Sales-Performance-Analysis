# Analysis Findings — Sales Performance

## Dataset scope
24 synthetic orders from January–June 2025; four regions, four sales representatives and three product categories. Values are illustrative, not real company results.

## KPI results
- Net revenue after discounts: **$22,841.75**
- Units sold: **165**
- Orders: **24**
- Average order value: **$951.74**
- Highest-revenue region/representative: **North / Ada — $7,678.00**
- Highest-revenue category: **Electronics — $13,140.50** (about 57.5% of revenue)

## Monthly revenue
| Month | Net revenue |
|---|---:|
| Jan 2025 | $4,854.50 |
| Feb 2025 | $3,724.00 |
| Mar 2025 | $3,209.50 |
| Apr 2025 | $3,548.75 |
| May 2025 | $3,265.25 |
| Jun 2025 | $4,239.75 |

January was the strongest month, while March was the lowest. Revenue recovered in June.

## Recommendations
1. Investigate the drivers behind January's peak and March's dip before setting targets.
2. Review the strong Electronics contribution and assess whether cross-selling accessories can lift order value.
3. Compare discount levels with unit volume and margin; revenue alone cannot establish profitability.
4. Review regional performance in context of territory size and sales opportunity.

## Limitations
Small synthetic sample, no cost/margin data, no customer IDs and no targets. These results demonstrate analysis technique, not business performance.
